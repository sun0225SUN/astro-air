If I love you, and you happen to love me.

Then when you are sick, I will take care of you and stay with you until you recover.

If I love you, and you happen to love me.

I will ask you to be careful when you ride your bike, and call me when you arrive.

When you forget to eat dinner, I will pretend to be angry and say that you are making me worried.

When your hair is messy, I will smooth it out for you with a smile, and then I will linger on your hair for a few more seconds.

If you want to cry, I will cry with you, even though I was in a happy mood just a moment ago.

If you want to laugh, I will laugh out loud with you, no matter that I was actually depressed the last second.

When I am shopping, I will think, ah, you just need this...

When I discover something good, I will immediately think of it and ask you to come and take a look.

When I stay up late, when I receive a call from you just to say "Don't be too tired, go to bed early", I will smile sweetly and go to sleep obediently.

After I lost sleep, I would secretly giggle in my heart when I heard that you also lost sleep.

When I am free, I will read your name and think about your voice.

When I am thinking of you, I know that you are also thinking of me.

But... if I love you and you unfortunately don't love me.

Then when you are sick, I will only call you to express my condolences to you, but I don’t dare to expect to be by your side.

But... if I love you and you unfortunately don't love me.

When you ride a bicycle, I will only secretly hope that you are safe.

If you forgot to eat dinner, I would just smile and ask, why don't you eat it?

Your hair is messy, I will only tell you gently, your hair is messy.

If you want to cry, I can only stay by your side and sigh softly.

You want to smile, but I can only smile slightly at you.

When I was shopping, I would think about who bought this for you.

When I discover something good, I will helplessly wonder who will tell you this good news.

When I stay up late, I don't dare expect the phone to ring.

After I suffer from insomnia, I will hide from you to prevent you from seeing the dark circles under my eyes.

When I have free time, I still read your name and think about your voice.

When I am thinking about you, I will think about who you are thinking about at this time.

If I don't love you anymore, I will definitely not love you anymore and I will fall in love with someone else.

Is there anything in the world that cannot be lost? I believe there is, and you'd better believe it too.
