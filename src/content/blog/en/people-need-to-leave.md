Actually, I don’t talk much about travel or tourism. One word I often use is “run away”. When a person stays in an environment for too long and becomes too familiar, he loses his sensitivity and the stimulation of his creativity, so he needs to leave.

## Don’t ask what to prepare, ask what you love first

I hope that "Grand Tour" will encourage young people to go out and make a living. If you can't make a breakthrough today, you won't be able to make a difference in the future. Many people who want to go to Europe will think that I have been in Europe for a long time, so they will ask me: "I want to go to Europe, what should I prepare?" I will ask them: "What do you think you are going to do?"

When you know what you want to do and have strong willpower, all difficulties can be overcome step by step.

The preparation of our children today, their credit cards and Chinese skills are definitely better than those of the Taiwanese businessmen who went to Europe with product samples back then, but they just can't get out because they don't feel safe. Some people have even been asking for several years, but in the end they just can’t get out.

I often tell my friends that Sun Wukong in "Journey to the West" is so powerful that he can travel thousands of miles in a single somersault, so wouldn't it be easy for him to go and learn the scriptures? Why did Tang Sanzang learn scriptures? Because Sun Wukong has no motive, but Tang Sanzang has motive, although he does not have the ability to learn scriptures. But motivation is more important than ability. Without motivation, there is no starting point at all, not even a starting point. As long as there's motivation, it's great. What I fear most is not loving anything.

If a young person wants to go out, I will ask him: "What do you love?" If you like rock, want to play heavy metal, or want to join a band, I think that's fine. In addition, the word "Zhuang" in "Zhuangyou" is not just for showing off. The word "strong" contains a profound and non-biased dialogue with the local culture.

## Correct yourself in a different culture

All good travel books will have this point of view. Zhou Daguan, who wrote "The Customs of Chenla" and was envoy to Angkor Thom, was a northerner in the Yuan Dynasty, so when he went south, he couldn't stand the weather. He didn't understand how the locals took baths multiple times a day.

A year later, he changed. At the beginning, he had the mentality of a big country. The Yuan Dynasty was so great at that time, but he later said that Chenla (today's Angkor Wat in Cambodia), a small Southeast Asian country, had such strict etiquette and "should not be underestimated." I think it is impossible for people to be subjective, but only by slowly correcting your own prejudices and subjectivity while traveling can you achieve good travel.

Even if you just join a tour group, you can have a different experience and perspective. Information is really developed now. Before setting off, you can do some preparation work. Second, after arriving at the scene, try to review your own subjectivity.

When I take my friends to Angkor Wat, I will say: "I will take you to a Cambodian home now." They will be shocked when they get out of the car. There is really nothing there. We call them "house disciples with four walls", but they don't even have walls. I'm in Taiwan, and I always feel like I'm still missing something.

I have never had such an experience in my life. I think this is a big gain. So I think any trip is worth it, because as long as you compare it, you will come back to review the meaning and value of your life.

Traveling is not just about seeing, it is about finding the most beautiful things within yourself. The external scenery is actually your own mood. Therefore, Zhuangyou is definitely not just an outward observation, but an inward reflection.

## Start with a simple change

Traveling is of course a great choice. If it is not possible in the short term... reading, handicrafts, chatting, learning, companionship, sharing, exercising, relaxing, singing, painting... are also very good methods. As long as it can change the proportion of your life, it will naturally change the quality of your life and avoid brain rigidity and soul numbness.

How long has it been since you looked up at the sky, looked at the flowers and grass on the roadside, and listened to the birds chirping on the street trees? Start with this simple change!
