The asphalt road undulates constantly, as if the road is sticking to the waves. I am walking on this mountain road, I am like a boat. I am eighteen years old this year, and the few yellow beards on my chin are fluttering in the wind. They are the first beards to settle here, so I cherish them very much. I have walked on this road for a whole day and have seen many mountains and many clouds. All the mountains and clouds remind me of familiar people. I called them their nicknames, so even though I walked all day, I was not tired at all. I passed through the morning in this way, and now entered the end of the afternoon, and I also saw the hair of dusk. But I haven't entered a hotel yet.

I met many people on the road, but they didn't know where they were and whether there was a hotel ahead. They all told me: "Go and take a look." I thought what they said was very good, and I actually walked over and took a look. But I haven't entered a hotel yet. I feel like I should worry about the hotel.

I was surprised that I only encountered a car once after walking all day. It was noon, and I just wanted to hitchhike. But at that time, I just wanted to hitchhike. I didn't worry about the hotel at that time. At that time, I just thought it was great to get a ride. I stood on the side of the road and waved to the car. I tried my best to wave gracefully. But the driver didn't even look at me, and the car, like the driver, didn't even look at me. It just passed in front of my eyes. I chased after the car for a while, just for the fun of it because I didn't have to worry about the hotel at that time. I chased the car until it disappeared, and then I laughed to myself, but I soon realized that laughing too hard would affect my breathing, so I stopped laughing immediately. Then I continued walking happily, but I began to regret in my heart that I didn't put a big stone in my hand while waving it coolly.

Now I really wanted to take a ride, because dusk was coming, but the hotel was still in its mother's belly, but I didn't see another car all afternoon. If I were to stop another car now, I think I would be able to stop it. I would lie down in the middle of the road and I'm sure all the cars would slam on their brakes right next to my ears. But now I can't even hear the sound of the car's motor. Now I can only walk over and take a look. That’s right. Go over and take a look. "

The road has ups and downs, and the high point always tempts me, tempting me to rush up to see the hotel, but every time I only see another high point, with a depressing arc in the middle. Despite this, I still ran to higher places again and again, and every time I ran to death. Now I'm running to higher ground again. This time I saw it, not a hotel but a car. The car was parked in my direction, at a low point on the road. I saw the driver’s buttocks raised high, with the sunset glow on his buttocks. I couldn't see the driver's head, which was stuck in the front of the car. The front cover of the car tilted up, like lips turned up. There were baskets piled high in the carriage, and I thought they must be filled with fruits. Of course bananas are the best. I think there should be some in his cab, so I can pick it up and eat it as soon as I sit in. Although the car will drive in the direction I came from, I no longer care about the direction. I need a hotel now. If I don’t have a hotel, I need a car. The car is right in front of me.

I ran over happily and greeted the driver: "Hello, fellow man. "

The driver didn't seem to hear it and was still doing something.

"Fellow, smoke. "

Only then did he use all his strength to pull the head out, and stretched out a dark hand to grab the cigarette I handed over. I quickly lit a fire for him. He put the cigarette in his mouth and took a few puffs, then put his head in it again.

So I felt at ease, as long as he took my cigarette, he would let me ride in his car. I wandered around the car to detect the contents of the basket. But I couldn't see clearly, so I smelled it with my nose and smelled apples. Apples were not bad, I thought.

After a while he repaired the car, closed the hood and jumped out. I quickly walked up and said, "fellow, I want a ride. Unexpectedly, he pushed me with his black hand and said roughly: "Get away." "

I was so angry that I had nothing to say, but he slowly opened the car door and got in, and then the engine started to sound. I know if I miss this opportunity, I will never get another chance. I know I should take the plunge now. So I ran to the other side, opened the car door and got in. I was ready to have a fight with him in the cab. When I entered, I first yelled at him: “You still have my cigarette in your mouth. "By this time the car had moved.

However, he looked at me with a friendly smile, which puzzled me. He asked: "Where are you going?"

I said, "Wherever you want. "

He asked kindly: "Want to eat an apple?" He still looked at me.

“Then there’s no need to ask. "

“Go get it in the back. "

He was driving the car so fast. How dare I climb out of the cab and crawl into the back? So I said, "Forget it." "

He said, “Go get it. "His eyes were still looking at me.

I said, “Don’t look, there’s no road on my face. "

Only then did he turn his head to look at the road.

The car drove in the direction I came from. I sat comfortably on the seat, looked out the window, and chatted with the driver. Now he and I have become friends. I already knew he was trafficking individually. The car is his, and so is the apple. I also heard the money jingling in his pocket. I asked him: "Where are you going?"

He said: "Drive over and take a look. "

These words almost sounded like what my brother would say, so kind. I feel closer to him. Everything outside the car window should be familiar to me. Those mountains and clouds reminded me of another group of familiar people, so I called out another group of nicknames.

Now I don't care about the hotel at all, this car, this driver's seat makes me feel at ease and at ease. I had no idea where the car was going and neither did he. Anyway, it doesn't matter where we are in front of us. As long as the car is driving, let's go and take a look.

But the car broke down, and by that time we were already very good friends. I put my hand on his shoulder and he put his hand on mine. He was telling me about his love affair, and when he was about to talk about the feeling of embracing a woman for the first time, the car broke down. The car broke down while going uphill. At that time, the car suddenly stopped barking and stopped moving like a dead pig. So he climbed onto the front of the car again, turned up his upper lip again, and stuffed his head in again. I was sitting in the cab, and I knew his butt must be raised high at the moment, but his upper lip blocked my view. I couldn't see his butt, but I could hear the sound of him repairing the car.

After a while he pulled out the head and closed the hood of the car. His hands were even darker at that time. He wiped his dirty hands on his clothes again and again, then jumped on the ground and walked over.

"Repaired?" I asked.

“It’s over, there’s no way to fix it. " he said.

I finished thinking, "What should I do?" I asked.

“We’ll see. ” he said nonchalantly.

I'm still sitting in the car, wondering what to do. Now I think of the hotel again. The sun was setting at that time, and the sunset was rising like steam. The hotel came back to my mind, and gradually expanded, and soon it filled my head. At that time, the iron head was gone, and a hotel grew in its place.

The driver started doing radio exercises in the middle of the highway. He went from the first quarter to the last quarter and did it very seriously. After finishing, he started trotting around the car again. The driver may have been in the cab for too long and now needs some exercise. Seeing him moving outside, I couldn't sit still inside, so I opened the car door and jumped out. But I didn’t do any hands-off exercises or trot. I was thinking about hotels and inns.

At this time, I saw five people riding bicycles down the slope. Each bicycle had two large baskets tied to a pole on the back seat. I thought they were probably farmers nearby, returning from selling vegetables. I was very happy when I saw someone coming down, so I went up to greet them and shouted: "Hello, fellow villagers. "

When the five rode up to me, they jumped out of the car. I happily greeted them and asked, "Is there a hotel nearby?"

Instead of answering, they asked me, "What's in the car?"

I said, "It's Apple. "

The five of them pushed their bicycles to the car. Two of them climbed on to the car, and then they turned down ten baskets of apples. The three people below opened the lids of the baskets and poured them into their own baskets. I didn't know what happened for a while, and the scene left me dumbfounded. When I understood, I rushed forward and asked, "What are you going to do?"

None of them paid any attention to me and continued pouring apples. I went up and grabbed the hand of one of them and shouted: "Someone is grabbing the apple!" At this time, a fist hit me hard on the nose and I was knocked several meters away. When I got up and touched it with my hands, I found that my nose was not attached to my face, but hanging on my face. The blood flowed like sad tears. But when I saw the strong blacksmith clearly, the five of them had already mounted their bicycles and started riding away.

The driver was walking slowly at the moment, his lips rolled up and he was gasping for air. He was probably tired from running just now. He didn't seem to know what happened just now. I shouted to him: "Your apple has been taken away!" But he didn't pay attention to what I was shouting and was still walking slowly. I really wanted to go up and punch him and make his nose hang up. I ran over and shouted into his ear: "Your apple was taken away. "He then turned around and looked at me. I found that his expression was getting happier and happier, and I found that he was looking at my nose.

At this time, many people came down the slope on bicycles. There were two large baskets behind each bicycle, and there were some children among the cyclists. They swarmed in and immediately surrounded the car. Many people jumped on top of the car, and baskets of apples fell down one after another. The apples flowed out from some broken baskets like my nosebleeds. They were all frantically filling their baskets with apples. In just a moment, all the apples on the car were on the ground. At that time, several walking tractors rumbled down the slope. The tractors also stopped next to the car. A group of big men jumped out and started loading apples onto the tractors. The empty baskets were thrown out one by one. At that time, the apples were already rolling all over the ground, and everyone was squatting like toads to pick up the apples.

It was at this time that I rushed forward regardless of my own safety. I yelled, "Bandit!" and rushed forward. So countless fists and feet came to greet me, and every part of my body was punched almost at the same time. As I stood up from the ground, several children hit apples at me. The apple hit his head and broke, but his head didn't break. I was about to rush over and beat those kids when a foot kicked me hard in the waist. I wanted to call out, but when I opened my mouth, there was no sound. I fell to the ground and couldn't get up anymore. I could only watch them snatching apples. I started to use my eyes to look for the driver. This guy was standing in the distance and laughing at me. I knew that my appearance now must be more exciting than the nose I had just now.

At that time I didn't even have the strength to be angry. I could only look at these things with my eyes, which made me extremely angry. What I was most angry about was the driver.

Some more tractors and bicycles came down the slope, and they also participated in the catastrophe. I saw fewer and fewer apples on the ground, and watched some people leaving and some coming. The late arrivals started to work on the car. I watched them remove the window glass, remove the tires, and pry off the wooden boards. The car looked particularly dejected after the tires were removed, lying on the ground. Some children went to pick up the baskets that had just been thrown out. I saw that the ground was getting cleaner and cleaner, and there were fewer and fewer people. But I could only watch at that time, because I didn’t even have the strength to be angry. I sat on the ground and couldn't get up. I could only let my eyes move around.

The area was deserted now, except for a tractor parked next to the lying car. Several people were looking around beside the car to see if there was anything else they could take away. After watching for a while, they climbed onto the tractor one by one, and the tractor started.

At this time, I saw the driver also jumped onto the tractor. He sat down in the truck and was still laughing at me. I saw that he was holding my red backpack. He snatched my backpack away. The backpack contains my clothes and my money, as well as food and books. But he snatched my backpack away.

I watched the tractor climb up the slope and then disappear, but I could still hear its sound, but after a while there was no sound at all. The surrounding area fell silent, and the sky began to darken. I was still sitting on the ground. I was hungry and cold, but now I had nothing.

I sat there for a long time, and then slowly got up. It was very difficult for me to get up, because every time I moved, my whole body was in severe pain, but I still got up. I limped to the car. The car looked really miserable. It was lying there covered in cuts and bruises. I knew that I was also covered in cuts and bruises.

It was completely dark, and there was nothing around except the bruised car and me. I looked at the car with infinite sadness, and the car looked at me with infinite sadness. I reached out and touched it. It was cold all over. At that time, the wind began to pick up, and the wind was very strong. The sound of the leaves shaking on the mountain was like the sound of sea waves. This sound frightened me and made me as cold as a car.

I opened the door and got in. They didn't pry the seat off, which gave me some comfort. I lay down in the cab. I smelled the smell of leaking gasoline, which was like the smell of blood flowing out of my body. The wind was getting stronger and stronger outside, but I started to feel a little warmer as I lay on the seat. I felt that although the car was covered in bruises, its heart was still sound and warm. I know my heart is warm too. I've been looking for a hotel, but I didn't expect you to be here.

Lying in the pit of my heart in the car, I remembered such a sunny and gentle noon, when the sunshine was very beautiful. I remember playing happily outside for a long time, and then I went home. I saw my father packing a red backpack outside the window. I threw myself at the window and asked, "Dad, are you going out?"

The father turned around and said gently: "No, I want you to go out."

"Let me go out?"

"Yes, you are already eighteen, you should get to know the outside world."

Later, I carried that beautiful red backpack, and my father patted me on the back of the head, like a pat on the horse’s butt. So I rushed out of the house happily and ran happily like a cheerful horse.
