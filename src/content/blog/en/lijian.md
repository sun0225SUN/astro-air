## Headline

The Spring Festival is here again, and hundreds of millions of Chinese people are rushing back to their hometowns.

What is hometown? That's the parents.

As the ancients said, if your parents are here, you won’t have to travel far; and in modern society where everyone is wandering, parents are the most important goal and meaning of this reunion festival.

The so-called hometown is where the parents are. What are we talking about when we talk about parents?

There is no standard answer to this question, nor should there be one. It is just a question from one era to another era, and a pursuit from one life to another. It's everyone asking everyone.

In 2015, this magazine invited more than ten people from all walks of life to write articles about their parents, recalling, describing or describing their parents in their eyes and hearts, and the meaning their parents brought to their lives.

Today, I push "Father Li Jiuliang: Many years after you passed away, I am proud of you" from "To Parents", written by Li Jian for this magazine.

Li Jian

singer. One of the original members of the group "Shui Mu Nian Hua". Graduated from the Department of Electronic Engineering of Tsinghua University, he is regarded as a "musical poet" by the outside world because of his rational, calm voice with classical temperament and his insistence that he does not please the market. In 2010, Faye Wong returned to the public eye again with a cover of her work "Legend"; in January 2015, in "I Am a Singer", she stunned the stage with her unique temperament. He once composed 2 songs specifically to commemorate his father.

## Letter from home

If it hadn't been for my recent move, I wouldn't have been able to recall these letters from home from the last century. In fact, it was a letter written to me by my family when I was studying in college about 20 years ago. It felt like it was as far away as a century, and it was so long that it was a little trance-like and almost unreal; but when I re-read these letters, everything in the past gradually became clear, as if it happened yesterday.

I was born and raised in Harbin, which is a wonderful city, and I still think so. People here like to talk about life, especially things that are out of their reach, or even metaphysical issues that are higher than life. Although this is not an economically developed city, people have a strong sense of happiness. They are keen on celebrating festivals and gatherings. In my memory, every family has a large population, and relatives and friends gather frequently during holidays, which makes me feel the ubiquitous family affection since I was a child.

Since it was a colonial city in its early years, people have continued their past living habits, loving to eat sausages, bread, drinking beer, and constantly renovating and decorating their houses. A few years ago, I went to Irkutsk, Russia for the first time. The city scene I saw seemed familiar, and I thought: Isn’t this the Harbin of my childhood?

There are countless literature and art lovers in Harbin. I think this is related to the beautiful scenery and cold weather. In the summer, there were many people painting landscapes by the Songhua River. At that time, I often squatted next to those painters and watched. I remember one time, a painter probably got impatient with me squatting next to him for a long time, and said, "Kid, do you understand?" I said loudly, "You don't paint well!" and ran away quickly. I had the impression that his paintings were very beautiful, but they just didn't match the scene in front of me. There are often people walking around the riverside wearing glasses and holding books thoughtfully in their hands. When I was a child, I called them artists. Later, I once thought that a person would not be very knowledgeable if he did not wear glasses. During that period, I even envied the short-sighted people in the class.

There were many monkey performers on the riverside. I don’t know why there were so many monkeys in that era, but one of the monkey performers actually wore a pair of glasses. This puzzled me for a long time.

Winter in Harbin is a real winter. The ice and snow bring me not only coldness, but also joy. Harbin used to have winter for half of the year, and all the words that describe winter can be found here. In my memory, winter means the crunching of feet in the snow and hands red from snowball fights; it means the white world that can be seen clearly even late at night; it means seeing slipping pedestrians on the street from time to time and the sound of snow being cleared one after another; it means the lifelike and dreamlike ice and snow sculptures along the Songhua River...

## Initial power

What Harbin has given me, in addition to the unique and beautiful scenery, is more of the friendship between people.

Both my parents came from large families, and when they were combined, their families became even larger. I remember one time when my great-grandmother celebrated her birthday, I counted more than 120 relatives, and this was just from my father’s branch. Relatives moved around frequently and got to know each other. I think people at that time were not very rich and did not have much savings. Otherwise, everyone would know each other's family well.

Influenced by family, the relationship between classmates is also very close, and students' parents are sometimes more familiar with each other than relatives. I don’t know what the relationship between children is like today. The relationship we established as classmates at that time was very deep and has endured to this day. Every time I return to Harbin, visiting old classmates is as essential as visiting relatives. On the day I went to Beijing to study at university after graduating from high school, I went to the train station to see off a large group of my relatives and friends. It may seem exaggerated now, but at the time, it was commonplace.

I still remember that evening. The sadness of parting and the surge of friendship made my heart surge and became the motivation for me to write my first song.

Since I had never left home before, when I first entered college, I was not used to living independently. I was always homesick, and looking forward to letters from home became an indispensable part of my campus life. Every day after school, I would look for my name on the table with piles of letters in the communication room. In fact, the content of each letter is roughly the same, and I always take the trouble to read it over and over again. In the letter from home, in addition to telling me to study hard and pay attention to my health, it also told me not to be afraid of spending money. In fact, I have never been a person who knows how to calculate money. Sometimes I am willing to treat guests to dinner or something, but every time I feel that I have spent too much, I will deeply blame myself. At that time, everyone's family situation was almost the same, and they were not too wealthy. Especially since we had three children at home, the process of raising them was like an uphill climb, which was somewhat strenuous. However, in the letters, my mother often revealed intentionally or unintentionally that the family's financial situation was very good, allowing me to study with peace of mind, and I also tried my best to find some things that might make them happy, and even described my own vision for the future.

In 1995, I went to Hong Kong to perform with the school art troupe. At that time, Hong Kong had not yet returned to China. Going to Hong Kong was like going abroad. Everything I saw was novel. In the letter, I described Hong Kong to my parents and told my mother that one of the universities liked me very much and that I might go there to study for graduate school in the future. My mother wrote in a letter saying that the news had excited the whole family for several days, and she also said that after returning from school, she would build the country well and catch up with Hong Kong in the future. At that time, I felt that this was like what a conscious party member said. In fact, my parents were not party members and had no ambition in politics. But for some reason, they often spoke from the standpoint of the country.

Later, perhaps because the times were developing too fast, my mother gradually began to have doubts about many social phenomena. Now she is a Buddhist. I wonder if Buddhism can solve her doubts. I have never asked her.

Tsinghua University is a great honor for an ordinary family, and this family honor given to me makes me feel even more responsible to protect it. This is also a school with heavy homework, especially our electronics department, which is famous for its high learning pressure. To be honest, my academic performance has been better than that of most people since I was a child, but in Tsinghua University, which is full of strong players, I basically have no advantage. It was then that I discovered that many things depend on talent, and hard work alone is not enough.

When I was in third grade, I started to get tired of studying, and I had a vague idea of ​​dropping out of school. I felt depressed all day long. I remember one day, when I was sorting out letters in my dormitory, I read the letters from home when I was a freshman. The joy, pride and promises from my parents made me so ashamed that I burst into tears. I thought to myself, I cannot embarrass my kind-hearted parents, I cannot dispel their newly established self-confidence in social life, and I cannot let my family be filled with sorrow. I secretly made up my mind that I must persevere until I graduate and get my degree.

Looking back, I should be grateful to those letters and my ordinary and warm family for giving me the initial strength. Facts have proved that many things are just a matter of one thought, and many results are only one step away.

## You are proud of me, but I have never been proud of you

There is a letter that seems very interesting now. At that time, I often performed in college and also wrote songs and composed music. My mother was worried that this would affect my studies. The letter wrote: "You should still focus on your studies now. Don't always think about being a singer. Those are just dreams and unrealistic. Our family is ordinary people. You have to learn a skill and find a good job after graduation." To be a talented person who is useful to the country, your parents don’t expect you to be famous and make money. "In fact, I just loved music at that time and was relatively active on campus. I never thought about music as a career, because as my mother said, such a career was too far away from a family like ours, and it was impossible for me to become someone who often appeared on TV. She also knows how difficult it is to make a living by singing, because my father is a Peking Opera actor, and she has seen the price of pursuing an artistic career.

When I was young, I always went to work with my father. When they were rehearsing, I would wander around in the courtyard of the Peking Opera Troupe. I was accompanied by flowers, butterflies and dragonflies, and a lot of time. Whenever my father comes to look for me, someone will always tell him that he just saw Li Jian running with a steamed bun in his hand - for some reason, I always had a steamed bun in my hand at that time. Maybe steamed buns were also a snack for children in that era.

My father is the most honest and kind-hearted person I have ever met. In today's terms, he is completely harmless. In my memory, my first impression of him was in early winter. I guess I was about three years old at the time. I remember standing on the bed, and my father was putting on my cotton pants and saying, "It's snowing, winter is coming." I still remember watching the heavy snow falling from the sky outside the window. That was also my first memory of snow. But in this memory, there is no feeling of cold at all.

A few years later, during a cold winter (in fact, Harbin’s winters are all cold), I often woke up in the middle of the night and found my father writing (usually he often read newspapers, but rarely wrote anything), sometimes covering his chest. I feel very strange. It turns out that the work unit has increased the wages of many actors, but there is no father. It is said that a person who gave gifts to the boss took up the quota that belonged to the father. The father wrote a letter to the superior department to complain. Because he was in a bad mood, he had stomach trouble. I think what my father cares about is not only the level of salary, but also the recognition of an actor's professional title and respect for art. That was the first time I felt his melancholy, and I can still remember his expression.

I no longer remember the outcome of this incident, nor do I remember when his melancholy dissipated. Ordinary people's families are like boats floating on the sea. Wind and rain can rock them at any time, but for me, what I feel more is the warmth in the boat.

When I graduated from junior high school, I was admitted to the best high school in the city, which is equivalent to Tsinghua University in middle school. One time, my father was going to Russia with his company to perform. On that day, my mother asked me to see him off at the train station. I was a little surprised. He used to go to the station by himself when he was on a business trip, because his father usually didn't talk much and never bothered me to do anything for him. Later I found out that my father wanted to show off his son in front of his colleagues. Because those uncles and aunts liked me very much when I was a child, but now that I haven’t seen them for many years and I have been admitted to the best high school, my father especially wants them to meet me. I still remember the look of satisfaction on my father's face when they praised me. It was then that I truly realized how proud he was of me. At the same time, I also found that he was a little older, and he was slightly different from the high-spirited Wu Sheng father in the past. My heart tightened slightly.

In my memory, my father only shed tears in front of me twice. Once when I came home from Beijing for vacation one year, I told my father that I brought a gift for my grandfather. He told me that my grandfather passed away, and I saw him shed tears. Another time, after he got cancer, he needed an operation. When my sister and I pooled together the money to pay the fee, he was so moved that he cried. He said that the children were sensible and he was not causing trouble to them. This made me, who was already anxious, feel like a knife.

I took out all the tens of thousands of dollars I had at the time, and I realized how important money is sometimes. Then his condition worsened, and in the last stage of his life, I sent him back to Harbin. On the train, he was already very weak. Every time he went to the bathroom, I had to support him or carry him on my back. I didn't sleep much all night. I remember when I was carrying him, he said, forgive dad. At that moment, I held back tears. He was so polite that he was even polite to his son, who he had carried since childhood, but I had only carried him a few times.

My father’s back was the place I was most familiar with. It was the warm paradise where I often slept during my childhood. Although I couldn't see his expression, I knew it was an expression I was familiar with. I knew the meaning of this simple sentence, which included guilt, gratitude, concern, and reluctance... At that time, my singing career was not making much progress, and he had been worried about my life. Years later, I would occasionally think of this scene, and I often couldn't let go when I thought of this sentence. Just like when I started writing, tears welled up in my eyes again.

I once wrote a song called "Father", which said: You are proud of me, but I have never been proud of you. I am always ashamed that you are so generous. I re-recorded this song last year and added a sentence at the end: I finally understand that many years after you left, I am proud of you. When talking about you... I know that what I am proud of him is his forbearance in life and his loyalty to his family.

Today, our three children all live in Beijing, and my mother travels between Harbin, Beijing and Hainan like a migratory bird. She seeks happiness in solitude and a life that allows her to live. After all, life is cruel. After my mother entered this age, she began to face more unexpected farewells. Her parents, brothers and sisters also experienced separation and death one after another. Sometimes I really worry about her when I think about it.

Now, whenever I achieve something, she will often say when she is happy, it would be great if your dad was still alive. A few days ago, she was watching my TV show. When I finished singing a song, she clapped her hands excitedly in front of the TV and shouted: Good, good, good! She told me these things as interesting things, and I was happy after hearing them, but then I felt a sense of sadness in my heart. Yes, it would be great if my father was still alive. Then she would not be the only one applauding. The two of them would have a heated discussion. I can even imagine the content of their conversation.

However, I can't imagine what my father would look like if he lived to this day. In my memory, his final appearance is much younger than that of my mother now.
